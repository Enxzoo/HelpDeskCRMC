# Routes

## Student
- `/dashboard_student.php` — student dashboard, chat, concerns, and staff-reply thread.
- `/api/get_student_concerns_with_replies.php` — concern and reply data for the authenticated student.
- `/api/submit_student_reply.php` — student follow-up reply endpoint.
- `/api/submit_feedback.php` — feedback endpoint for a resolved concern.

## Staff
- `/dashboard_staff.php` — office-scoped staff workspace.

## Admin
- `/dashboard_admin.php` — administration portal.
